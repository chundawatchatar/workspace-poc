## The leader key

Browsers keep a few keys for themselves (`Ctrl/Cmd+W`, `T`, `N` and tab switching), so every command has a **leader sequence** that works everywhere: press `Ctrl+B`, release, then the key. While the leader waits, the sidebar footer shows `Ctrl+B …`.

Most commands also have a direct `Ctrl+Shift` combination, and on macOS the familiar `⌘D`, `⌘B` and `⌘P` work too.

## Your shell keeps its keys

`Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+P`, `Ctrl+R` and the rest always reach the terminal. The one exception is `Ctrl+B`, the leader. Press it twice, `Ctrl+B Ctrl+B`, to send it to readline, vim or tmux, as you would in tmux itself.
